Why Specialist Matters

Most agencies treat GBP as a side service. We specialize exclusively in reinstatement and ranking for Google Business Profiles. Primary category is the single highest-weighted local pack factor. Reviews (quantity, recency, response rate) and profile completeness drive the next largest share. Keyword stuffing in the business name is a leading suspension trigger — we never recommend it.

We are not Google and we are not a Google partner. We follow Google’s published appeal process on your behalf: correct the cause first, prepare matching evidence, and submit once through Google’s appeals tool. Google sets the timeline and the outcome. We do not guarantee reinstatement.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does reinstatement take?

Google does not publish a review time. A soft restriction and a hard suspension are different, and the result depends on the policy cited and the documents. After a diagnostic we tell you what we can prepare and what only Google can decide. We do not promise a date or a result.

Do you guarantee reinstatement?

No. Google makes the final decision. We prepare the strongest possible case with correct documentation and policy alignment, then manage the process transparently. Success depends on the legitimacy of the business and the quality of evidence.

What ranking factors actually matter in 2026?

Practitioners most often point to primary category, proximity, profile completeness, review signals (especially recency and response), accurate hours, and the business website. Google has not published those as official weights. Keyword stuffing in the name risks suspension and is not a sustainable strategy.

Are you part of Google?

No. RestoreRankGBP is an independent consultancy. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by Google. Our work is to prepare a complete request and submit it through Google’s own appeals process.

What documentation is needed for an appeal?

Official business registration, license or tax certificates, and utility bills whose name and address match the profile. Storefront photos with permanent signage when applicable. We provide a precise checklist after reviewing your case.
