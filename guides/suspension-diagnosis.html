How to Diagnose a GBP Suspension

Soft vs hard. Where to look. What to fix first.

The first step is always to open the official Google Business Profile appeals tool while signed into the account that manages the profile. The tool shows the decision date, the reason, the linked policy, and whether the decision can be appealed.

Soft vs Hard Suspensions

  • Soft — Profile is restricted or has limited visibility. Often related to specific content or verification issues. Frequently faster to resolve once the cause is corrected.
  • Hard — Profile is fully disabled or removed. More serious policy or legitimacy concerns. Requires stronger documentation and more careful preparation.

Common Causes to Check Immediately

  • Keywords or location added to the business name
  • Virtual office, P.O. box, or mailbox address without proper customer-facing presence
  • Mismatch between profile name/address and official documents
  • Incorrect primary category or service-area settings
  • Duplicate profiles for the same business

Correct the underlying issue before submitting an appeal. Google reviews the profile as it stands when the request arrives. An appeal can typically be submitted only once, so preparation matters.
