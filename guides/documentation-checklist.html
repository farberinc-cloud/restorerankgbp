Appeal Documentation Checklist

Google’s appeals tool allows optional evidence. The strongest cases use multiple documents that reinforce the same facts. Every document’s business name and address must match the profile exactly.

Core Documents

  • Official business registration, articles of organization, or state business license
  • Tax certificate, EIN confirmation letter, or equivalent
  • Utility bill (electricity, phone, internet, water) in the business name at the listed address

Location Proof (Storefront Businesses)

  • Clear photos of permanent exterior signage showing the business name
  • Entrance and interior photos that demonstrate customer access

Additional Supporting Items

  • Professional licenses or certificates of insurance when relevant
  • Lease or property documents (if they match)
  • Website, social profiles, or other public presence that shows the same name and address

Prepare every file before opening the evidence form. Once the form is opened, it must typically be completed within 60 minutes.
