Restore Your Suspended Google Business Profile

Independent specialists for soft suspensions, hard suspensions, ownership issues, and verification problems. We submit the request through Google’s appeals process. Google makes the decision.

What We Handle

  • Soft suspensions (profile restricted or limited visibility)
  • Hard suspensions (profile fully disabled or removed)
  • Ownership disputes and access recovery
  • Verification failures and advanced verification issues
  • Policy mismatches (name, address, category, service-area rules)

Our Process

  1. Diagnostic — Review the appeals tool decision, cited policy, and current profile state. Identify the root cause before any appeal is prepared.
  2. Correction — Align the profile with Google’s guidelines. Fix name, address, categories, or operational model so the appeal is made on a compliant listing.
  3. Evidence package — Prepare matching documents: business registration or license, tax certificates, utility bills, and (when relevant) storefront photos with permanent signage. Name and address must match the profile.
  4. Appeal submission — Submit once through the official Google Business Profile appeals tool, with supporting evidence attached within the required window.
  5. Monitoring & follow-up — Track status and respond to any additional requests. Post-reinstatement health check to reduce re-suspension risk.

Realistic Timelines

Google does not publish review times and does not guarantee reinstatement. Some restrictions are simpler than others. After the diagnostic we explain what the file needs and what remains Google’s decision. We never promise that a profile will be restored.

Documentation Checklist Overview

Strong appeals combine multiple reinforcing documents:

  • Official business registration, articles, or state license
  • Tax certificates or EIN confirmation
  • Utility bill (electric, phone, internet) in the business name at the address
  • Storefront photos showing permanent signage matching the business name (for address-visible profiles)
  • Any professional licenses or insurance certificates that match the operating model

Every document’s name and address must match the profile exactly. We supply a precise, case-specific list after review.

After Reinstatement

We complete a safe optimization sequence so the newly restored profile does not immediately re-trigger issues. This includes category review, completeness, review response hygiene, and monitoring recommendations. Ongoing protection is available if desired.
