What Actually Moves Rankings in 2026

Local-search practitioners, including the Whitespark 2026 survey of 47 experts, discuss these as influential. They are not weights published by Google:

  • Primary GBP category — single highest-weighted factor
  • Proximity to the searcher (second highest)
  • Profile completeness and accurate information
  • Review signals — quantity, recency, rating quality, and response rate (rising in importance)
  • Being open at the time of search (now a top-five factor)
  • Additional categories, services, attributes, posts, and on-page website signals

Keyword stuffing in the business name remains a common suspension trigger and is not a sustainable ranking tactic. We optimize within Google’s guidelines.

Our Optimization Sequence

  1. Audit — Current category selection, completeness score, review velocity, hours accuracy, website-GBP consistency, and competitive gaps.
  2. Category & relevance — Set the most specific primary category. Add relevant secondary categories. Complete services, attributes, and description with natural language.
  3. Prominence signals — Review acquisition hygiene, response protocols, post cadence, and photo quality.
  4. Website tie-in — NAP consistency, local schema, service pages, and content that supports the same categories.
  5. Measurement — Ranking grids, call tracking, and impressions. Clear before/after reporting.

Safe Post-Reinstatement Optimization

If your profile was recently restored, we use a controlled sequence that improves visibility without re-triggering policy issues. No sudden keyword additions to the name, no aggressive review velocity that looks unnatural, and full documentation of every change.

Common Mistakes That Trigger Re-Suspension

  • Adding keywords or location to the business name
  • Virtual office or mailbox addresses without proper signage and staff presence
  • Inconsistent NAP across the web and documents
  • Rapid, unnatural review spikes immediately after reinstatement
  • Multiple profiles for the same business at the same location
