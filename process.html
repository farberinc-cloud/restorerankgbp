Our Documented Process

Transparent steps. No hype. Clear communication at every stage.

1. Diagnostic & Case Review

We review the suspension decision (or ranking baseline), the cited policy, current profile state, and supporting documents you can provide. You receive a clear read on the issue and a recommended path.

2. Documentation or Optimization Audit

For reinstatement: precise evidence package with matching name/address. For ranking: full audit of categories, completeness, reviews, website consistency, and competitive gaps.

3. Submission or Implementation

The appeal is filed once through Google’s appeals tool, or ranking changes are made in a sequence that stays inside Google’s guidelines. You receive a summary of every action taken. Filing a request is not the same as Google approving it.

4. Monitoring & Follow-up

Status tracking, response to any Google requests, and post-recovery or post-optimization health check.

5. Ongoing Protection (Optional)

Monthly management to monitor for issues, maintain ranking signals, and respond quickly if anything changes.
