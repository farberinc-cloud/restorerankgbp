MASTER AGREEMENT

General Retainer

Google Business Profile services. No guarantee of reinstatement or rank.

Form November 2026 · Effective upon the first monthly payment · US $1,200 per month

This Agreement takes effect when the Client pays the first monthly retainer or directs RestoreRankGBP to begin, whichever happens first. Each later payment confirms it again. Read it before paying. Payment is the Client’s signature.

1. Parties and status

This General Retainer (the “Agreement”) is between RestoreRankGBP (“Consultant”) and the person or business that requests the services and pays the retainer (“Client”). Consultant is an independent consultancy. Consultant is not Google. Consultant is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by Google, and is not the Client’s attorney, employee, agent, or partner. Google is not a party to this Agreement and owes nothing under it. Nothing Consultant files, writes, or says is a statement by Google.

2. Services

Consultant will perform professional services on the Google Business Profile the Client identifies. The scope is reinstatement, ranking, or both, as agreed when the engagement starts. The work may include diagnosis, corrections to align the profile with Google’s published guidelines, preparation of matching evidence, one submission through Google’s appeals process when reinstatement is the job, and ranking changes that stay inside those guidelines. Consultant does not operate Google’s systems and does not control reviewers, policies, or results. The engagement continues for as long as it takes to restore the profile or to complete the agreed ranking work, unless it ends sooner under Section 8.

3. Monthly fee

The Client shall pay Consultant one thousand two hundred dollars (US $1,200) per month, due at the start of each month, for as long as the engagement continues. The fee buys time and professional services. It does not buy a result. A month that has started is earned when it starts and is not refundable. Consultant may pause work if a payment is late, without extending any timeline.

4. Payment shows satisfaction of the previous month

Any monthly payment shows satisfaction of the previous month’s work. By paying a monthly retainer after a month of work, the Client acknowledges that every service performed in the immediately preceding month was satisfactory, accepts that work in full, and releases Consultant from any claim, demand, damage, or liability arising out of that previous month, to the fullest extent permitted by law.

The first payment is acceptance of this Agreement. It is not a review of earlier work. If the Client is not satisfied with a month, the Client’s only remedy is to stop the retainer in writing before the next payment is due. Paying the next month waives the objection. Consultant has no duty to keep working into a month the Client has declined to pay.

5. No guarantees

Consultant makes no guarantee, warranty, or representation of reinstatement, of any ranking or map-pack position, of calls, leads, or revenue, of a date, or of any act or omission by Google. A profile may remain suspended or restricted. Visibility may not change, and it may get worse. Google may change its policies, tools, or decisions at any time, including after a submission. All services are provided “as is” and “as available.” To the fullest extent permitted by law, Consultant disclaims every warranty, express or implied, including merchantability, fitness for a particular purpose, title, and non-infringement.

6. Travel

Travel happens only if the case requires it and only after the Client agrees to that trip in writing. In addition to the monthly retainer, the Client pays (a) all travel expenses at cost, including airfare, lodging, ground transportation, and related costs of the trip, and (b) a per diem of seven hundred dollars (US $700) for each day spent traveling or on site, including the day of departure and the day of return. The per diem is in addition to the expenses, not instead of them. Travel charges are earned when the travel occurs and are not refundable.

7. Client’s authority and duties

The Client represents that the business is real and lawfully operating; that the Client owns the profile or has the owner’s authority to hire Consultant; that every fact and document the Client supplies is genuine and matches the business; and that the Client will not ask Consultant to violate Google’s guidelines, including by adding keywords or a city to the business name. The Client is solely responsible for the truth of the profile and the documents. Consultant may refuse or stop any task that would misstate facts or breach those guidelines. Refusal is not a breach, and it does not refund the current month.

8. Term and stopping

The retainer runs month to month for as long as it takes to restore the profile or to finish the agreed ranking work. Either party may stop the engagement by written notice, including email, before the next billing date. Work stops when the retainer stops. Stopping does not refund a month already started, does not undo satisfaction of a month already paid, and does not require Consultant to withdraw a request already submitted to Google.

9. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, Consultant’s total liability arising out of this Agreement or the services, whether in contract, tort, negligence, strict liability, or any other theory, shall not exceed the retainer actually paid for the single month in which the claim arose. If that month has been satisfied under Section 4, no further sum is owed for it. Consultant is not liable for indirect, incidental, special, consequential, punitive, or exemplary damages, or for lost profits, lost revenue, lost calls, lost visibility, lost rankings, or loss of business, even if advised that such damages were possible. These limits are a bargained part of the fee. They survive the end of the engagement. If a limit is held unenforceable, it is enforced to the maximum extent the law allows, and the Client’s sole remedy is to stop future months under Section 8.

10. Release and indemnity

The Client releases Consultant from every claim arising out of Google’s decisions, delays, suspensions, reinstatements, ranking changes, account actions, or policy changes. The Client shall indemnify, defend, and hold Consultant harmless from any claim, loss, damage, or expense, including reasonable attorneys’ fees, arising out of the Client’s business, the profile, documents or information the Client supplied, the Client’s lack of authority, a third party’s rights, or the Client’s breach of this Agreement.

11. Assumption of risk

The Client understands that a Google Business Profile is controlled by Google, can be suspended or ignored without notice, and may never return to its former visibility. The Client chooses to proceed with that risk. The retainer is not insurance, not a bond, and not a purchase of a ranking or a reinstatement.

12. Time to raise a claim

To the fullest extent permitted by law, the Client must send any claim in writing within thirty (30) days after the last day of the month in which the services at issue were performed. A claim not sent in that time is waived. Payment of the next month is a separate waiver under Section 4, even if thirty days have not passed.

13. Entire agreement

This document is the entire agreement about these services. It replaces every prior discussion, estimate, and representation. A change is binding only if both parties confirm it in writing. If one sentence is held invalid, the rest stays in effect, and that sentence is cut back only as far as required. A failure to enforce a term is not a waiver of it. The Client may not assign this Agreement without Consultant’s written consent. Consultant may use subcontractors and remains subject to this Agreement if it does.

14. Notices and governing law

Notices may be sent by email to the addresses the parties use for the engagement and are effective when sent. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the state in which Consultant principally operates, without regard to conflict-of-law rules. Subject to the waivers in this Agreement, the state and federal courts sitting in that state are the exclusive forum. The Client consents to personal jurisdiction there.
