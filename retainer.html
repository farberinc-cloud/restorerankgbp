General Retainer

$1,200 per month. No guarantees. The work continues for as long as it takes.

What the retainer covers

The retainer is for ongoing specialist work on one Google Business Profile: reinstatement, ranking, or both, as agreed at the start. It pays for the work. It does not pay for a result.

  • Diagnosis of a suspension, restriction, or ranking gap
  • Corrections needed to align the profile with Google’s published guidelines
  • Preparation of matching evidence and one submission through Google’s appeals process, when reinstatement is the job
  • Ranking work that stays inside those guidelines, when ranking is the job
  • Status updates while the engagement is active

RestoreRankGBP is an independent consultancy. We are not Google, and we are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by Google. Google decides whether a profile is restored and where it ranks.

Fee and term

  • $1,200 USD per month, billed at the start of each month
  • The retainer continues for as long as it takes to restore the profile, or to complete the agreed ranking work
  • It ends when that work is finished, or when either party stops it in writing before the next billing date
  • If the retainer stops, the work stops. Months already started are not refunded

No guarantees

There is no guarantee of reinstatement, of a ranking position, of a timeline, or of any particular action by Google. A suspended profile may stay suspended. A profile may not move in the map pack. The monthly fee is owed whether or not Google grants the request.

Travel

Travel is used only when the case requires it, and only after the client agrees to the trip in writing. The client pays:

  • All travel expenses, including airfare, lodging, ground transportation, and other costs of the trip
  • A per diem of $700 USD per day for each day spent traveling or on site

The per diem is in addition to the travel expenses and in addition to the monthly retainer. Expenses are billed at cost. The per diem is billed for every travel day, including departure and return days.

Before work starts

A case review confirms the scope: restore, rank, or both. The first month is due before work begins. Nothing on this page changes Google’s policies or creates an obligation for Google to act.
