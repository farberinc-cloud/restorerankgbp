Specialist Expertise

RestoreRankGBP is an independent specialist consultancy for Google Business Profile reinstatement and ranking. We are not Google. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or certified by Google. We are also not a general agency that treats this as a side service.

What we do is specific: we learn why a profile was restricted, bring it in line with Google’s published guidelines, prepare evidence whose name and address match the profile, and submit one request through Google’s appeals tool. Google reviews that request and decides whether to restore the profile. We cannot make that decision, and we do not claim that we can.

Our approach is calm, precise, and documentation-driven. We follow Google’s published guidelines and appeal process exactly. We do not claim guaranteed outcomes because Google makes the final decision. What we do claim is a clear process, plain updates, and recommendations that stay inside the guidelines.

Primary category selection, profile completeness, review signals (especially recency and response), and accurate operational information are the levers we control. Keyword stuffing and other guideline violations are never part of our recommendations.

Based in the United States. Serving businesses nationwide that cannot afford prolonged invisibility.
